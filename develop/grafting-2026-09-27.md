# An exact grafting theorem and subcubic trees with unbounded gap

Let D be the six-vertex double star: centers u,v; leaves a,b at u and c,d
at v. Then beta_2(D)=4 and psi_sq(D)=5. An admissible five-coloring of D
colors every vertex, gives u,v one shared color, and gives the four leaves
four distinct colors.

## Theorem

For an arbitrary finite nonempty graph F with k vertices h_1,...,h_k,
construct R(F) by adding disjoint copies D_i and edges h_i a_i.
Then

    beta_2(R(F)) = 4k + beta_2(F),
    psi_sq(R(F)) = 5k.

Proof of beta_2. Restriction to each D_i has size at most four, and
restriction to F has size at most beta_2(F). Conversely take an optimal
2-independent set in F and {u_i,b_i,c_i,d_i} in each copy. No selected
vertex a_i meets F, and the only selected edges inside a copy are u_i b_i.
This attains the upper bound.

Proof of psi_sq. Leaving F uncolored and using five separate colors per
copy gives 5k. For any admissible coloring, its restriction to D_i remains
admissible: only a_i loses a neighbor, after which it has degree at most
one and automatically satisfies the condition. Write c_i<=5 for the
number of colors appearing in D_i and let r be the number of colors
appearing only in F. Choose one representative h_i for each such color;
these representatives have distinct indices. For a representative h_i,
c_i=5 would force the five-coloring described above. Then a_i would have
two colored neighbors, u_i and h_i, neither of its own color, which is
impossible. Thus each representative forces c_i<=4. Consequently

    number of colors <= sum_i c_i + r <= 5k-r+r = 5k.

This proves the theorem for every F, with no acyclicity assumption on F.
The colors shared between different copies only decrease the union count.

## Corollary: maximum degree three suffices

Take F=P_k. The result is a tree on 7k vertices with maximum degree three.
Since beta_2(P_k)=ceil(2k/3),

    beta_2(R(P_k)) = 4k + ceil(2k/3),
    psi_sq(R(P_k)) = 5k,
    psi_sq(R(P_k)) - beta_2(R(P_k)) = floor(k/3).

The path identity follows by selecting two of every three consecutive
vertices. Every three consecutive vertices contain at most two selected
vertices, which proves the matching upper bound.

The gap is therefore unbounded even among subcubic trees. More generally,
within the grafted family R(F), equality holds exactly when beta_2(F)=k,
equivalently when F itself has maximum degree at most one.

## Verification and provenance

The all-input result is proved above. The exact tree recurrence in
`tree_parameters.py` independently checks grafting over all 145 labelled
trees with 2..5 vertices and the path family for k=1..30. This extends the
earlier one-hub tree construction. The theorem is not yet Lean formalized.
Comparison with the caterpillar literature cited in arXiv:2609.25128v1 is
still needed before making a historical priority claim.
