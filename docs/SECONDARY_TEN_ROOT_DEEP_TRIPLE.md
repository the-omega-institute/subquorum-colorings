# First deep root triple: all actual t0 owners and one nonreused ledger

21 October 2026. Use the full H3 chain and explicit every-original full
even U/standard-rotor model of the
[mandatory-footprint theorem](SECONDARY_TEN_MANDATORY_E_FOOTPRINT.md).
The [first deep root pair](SECONDARY_TEN_ROOT_DEEP_PAIR.md) and its arbitrary
actual E-owner classification have independent written-review ACCEPT.
This note extends that pair to EVERY actual owner of the root t0 support.
No exhaustive component catalogue for arbitrary grids is assumed proved.

## Statement

Let n>=2, odd a=a0, even s=s0, even widths wj>=12, depths zj>=24 and
root breadth h>=6. Retain every H3 original with recurrence
a_(j+1)=a_j+z_j-6, s_(j+1)=s_j-w_(j+1)-12. Put u=w0,v=w1,z=z0,
C=s+u+13 and s1=s-v-12. Suppose a complete feasible extension contains
the specified actual original

    B=U(u+v+26,z+4,4), chart(a-14+r,s1-1+c).

The actual owner R of y0=(a-1,C) is necessarily

    R=U(K,8,g), chart(a-9+r,C-1+c),
    K,g even, K>=h+2, g>=4.

The actual owner A of t0=(a-4,s) is necessarily EXACTLY

    A=U(L,d,f), chart(a-4-d+r,s+1-c),
    L,d,f even, 6<=L<=v+12,
    d in {4,6,8}, 4<=f<=z+d-12.                       (1)

Conversely every listed A is endpoint-disjoint from all H3,B,R, for all
allowed R parameters. This converse asserts geometric compatibility only,
not complete extendibility. B,R,A are three distinct fresh originals.

Define epsilon=1 if L=v+12 and 0 otherwise; delta=1 if d8, else0. With
the original immutable charges, mixed full physical tiles and owner-labelled
intratile foreign events of the previous note, the exact selected-union
increments relative to H3 are

    DeltaS=u+v+L+24, DeltaN=1,
    DeltaF=8+epsilon*f/2                         if d4/6,
           L/2+7+epsilon*(f/2+1)                if d8,
    DeltaE=17+2*L*delta+epsilon*(f+1).

Consequently

    DeltaPhi({B,R,A}|H3)=u+v+L+24-epsilon        if d4/6,
                         u+v+22+epsilon        if d8,
                       >=46.                         (2)

In particular A's actual marginal after the pair is L-1-epsilon>=4
for d4/6 but -3+epsilon for d8. Therefore the positive pair receipt must
not be combined with an independently computed positive A receipt.
The exact joint ledger, rather than monotonicity, supplies compensation.

## Classification of every actual A

The accepted t-terminal classification applies unchanged: t0 is absent,
its old neighbors force its matching neighbor north, and the two permitted
full rotor roles hit P0 at(a-6,s+1). Every remaining role is a full north
terminal U in the left or right chart, with even L>=6,d,f>=4. Both
frames contain(a-13,s) when d>=10. That point belongs to B's row1 bar.
B does not contain t0, so these are distinct owners and cannot overlap.
Hence d is 4,6 or8.

Every right frame is excluded as follows.

- At d4 its bar hits P0 at(a-8,s+1).
- At d6/8 and L<=u+12 its far column F=s+L-1 lies in P0's row0 bar
  interval[s+1,s+u+12]. The far leg, with f>=4, reaches(a-8,F).
- At d6 and L>=u+14 its row1 bar reaches(a-9,C), an R near endpoint.
- At d8 and L>=u+14 its bar reaches(a-11,C), a B far endpoint.

Even widths leave no gap between the small and large alternatives. Only
the left frame survives. If L>=v+14, its bar spans B's near column s1;
the shared point(a-4-d,s1) belongs to both originals for d4/6/8.
Thus L<=v+12. Its far column F=s+1-L consequently lies in
[s1+1,s-5], inside P1's row0 bar column interval[s1+1,s].
That bar's row is a1-8=a+z-14. The A far stem starts at a-4-d and is
continuous to q=a-4-d+f. If f>=z+d-10, it hits that bar at(a+z-14,F).
The parity restrictions therefore force f<=z+d-12, equivalently
q<=a+z-16. This proves every necessary condition in(1).

For sufficiency, A's top bar rows are a-4-d,a-3-d and its near endpoint
is t0. Against its own old block, every full left consumer is admissible
as in the accepted entire-chain one-left-consumer proof. Its far column
is left of that block's minimum endpoint column s-4; the bar is above
all old first-block endpoints and the near stem is in the forced terminal
gap. The column bound F>=s1+1 puts the far stem in P1's first-contact
interval. The entire bar and both legs stop above the first P1 endpoints:
the far terminal is at most a+z-16, whereas P1 starts at a+z-14.
All other later originals start still lower. This also excludes any
same-tile contact with later blocks: the last possible A tile row ends
at a+z-16, and P1's first tile row starts at a+z-15.

B's bar ends at a-13, above A's first endpoint row a-12 or later.
Its near column s1 is strictly left of F and its far column C is
strictly right of every A endpoint (all have columns<=s). Thus there
is no endpoint collision with B. R's minimum endpoint column is C,
whereas A's maximum is s; C-s=u+13>=25 also separates their physical
tile columns. This proves sufficiency against every original and every
allowed R, independent of R's arbitrarily long far leg.

Distinctness needs no source assumption beyond the immutable originals:
B misses t0 and y0, R misses t0, and A misses y0. The original old support
classification makes each consuming original fresh. The same-color
terminal injection from the accepted footprint theorem also applies.

## Exact new receipts of A, partitioned into three disjoint sets

Use tile minima with even absolute coordinates. Write r0=a-4-d,
q=r0+f and F=s+1-L. In every case A's original charge is1-L; add its
mass L-1 once. Never recompute an old original's charge after its supports
are consumed. The pair contributes DeltaS=u+v+25, DeltaN1, DeltaF6,
DeltaE13 as previously proved.

### Own-P0 near receipt

There are exactly two new full tiles with minima(a-7,s),(a-5,s), and
four owner-labelled events: A-to-P0 and P0-to-A at each of rows a-7,a-5.
The immutable original lateral supports exclude the opposite parity rows;
the original axial terminal directions remain excluded. This contributes
2F-E=0, hence near marginal L-1 before the B interactions.

### Upper B/A bar band, only when d8

B's lower bar row a-13 is even; A's top row a-12 is odd. They are in
the SAME physical tile row. The overlap columns are F,F+1,...,s, giving
two directed events per column, with neither target an original support.
Thus there are exactly2L new bar events. The new full tile minima are

    (a-13,c), c=F+1,F+3,...,s-2,

exactly L/2-1 tiles. At either end of this list the remaining tile is
partial unless the tight far contact below supplies its missing cell.
This band's signed contribution is 2*(L/2-1)-2L=-L-2. Added to L-1,
it gives marginal -3 before the tight correction.

For d4/6 A starts in a later physical tile row and there is no upper-band
contact. B's bar cannot complete an A tile through any other old owner.

### Tight far contact, only when epsilon1

If L<v+12, parity gives F>=s1+3. B's near column s1 is in a different
physical tile column, and there is no far receipt. If L=v+12, F=s1+1
shares B's even physical tile column. All rows through q are within B's
incident column, since q<=a+z-16<a+z-10, its terminal row. The exact
new horizontal events are

    B-to-A at rows r0,r0+2,...,q;
    A-to-B at rows r0+1,r0+3,...,q-1.

The total is f+1. At r0 A's reverse direction targets its original
bar-exterior support; thereafter the full original lateral supports
give exactly the displayed alternation. These exclusions do not change
when a support is occupied by B.

The new full tile minima are(r,s1), r=r0+1,r0+3,...,q-1, totaling f/2.
At d8 there is ONE additional full tile with minimum(a-13,s1): B supplies
both upper cells and the lower-left cell, A supplies the lower-right cell.
It is distinct from the upper bar list, which begins at column s1+2.
Its new vertical events at column F are already in the bar-event list;
its new horizontal event is already in the far-event list. It is not
credited again to either list. Thus the tight signed correction is

    2*(f/2+delta)-(f+1)=2*delta-1.

### Exhaustion and nonreuse

Every physical tile containing A endpoints is on its bar or one of its
stems. Its own-P0 near strip is the first list. The bar sees B exactly
at d8, and its interior original full tiles are excluded by definition
from the mixed set F. Its far strip sees B only at the tight width.
Later blocks are separated by physical tile rows; R is separated by
physical tile columns. All remaining first-block endpoints are excluded
by the same own-block column/row separations as in the entire-chain left
theorem. There are no other foreign events or multicomponent completions.

The three full-tile lists have disjoint columns (or distinct rows), and
the three event lists are disjoint: near events have owners A/P0; bar
events A/B are vertical; tight far events A/B are horizontal. None was
present in H3+B+R, because every listed new event includes an A endpoint
and every listed full tile was missing an A cell. Conversely neither an
old event nor an old mixed tile disappears when a disjoint original is
added. The pair's bottom P0/R/E0 tile is remote and remains counted once.

Adding the pair and these three set partitions proves the exact increments
and(2) without assigning the same source mass or physical receipt twice.
For d4/6, L>=6,epsilon<=1 gives gain>=u+v+29>=53. For d8 the gain is
u+v+22+epsilon>=46. This proves the uniform all-parameter lower bound.

## A checkable failure of marginal monotonicity, not of the target

Take a31,s0,n2,u=v12,z0=z1=24,h6, R breadth8/far4 and A=L6,d8,f4.
All full original endpoint sets are disjoint. The pair gains49; the
triple gains46. Hence the exact marginal of this additional t0 consumer
is -3. The construction refutes nonnegative marginal for such selected
partial inventories; it does not refute the global target or prove that
this particular partial inventory has a complete feasible extension.
The stronger complete-family conclusion would require that separate
existence argument. Likewise(2) does not control arbitrary additional
owners or permit summing overlapping root triples along a chain.
