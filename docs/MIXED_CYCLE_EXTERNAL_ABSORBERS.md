# External absorber regions for arbitrary mixed U-port cycles

20 October 2026. For ANY finite family of closed components of the specified
neutral U-port graph, including mixed01/10 cycles and passive10 ports, the
external absorber-source families defined below are pairwise disjoint even
when the curves are nested. Internal absorption by selected phase10 sources
has one global two-terminal capacity. The resulting simultaneous inequality is

    M01+4ell <= 5M10+2B01+8Kext,                       (1)

where B01 counts only passive10 ports adjacent to phase01 ports, and Kext
counts actual absorber sources outside the chosen curve-source collection.
Both resource types are kept explicitly. This extends the previously proved
pure-cycle separation to mixed directions; it does NOT map Kext to reserve
or establish full coefficient-one allocation or rectangular-grid equality.

## The source families separate, including for mixed and nested curves

Each external owner's endpoint graph is connected and disjoint from every
chosen curve, because its original owner is not any chosen source. All source
and curve edges are unit lattice edges. The original inside forced point
therefore puts the entire source endpoint set strictly inside its generating
curve. Its supports lie inside or on that curve, with boundary supports consumed.

Let z be such a point generated by C and x=p+s its original anchor on C.
The unit edge xz has neither endpoint on another chosen curve D: x is on
C, and z is on no closed neutral curve. It cannot cross D without sharing
an integer endpoint. Thus z and x lie on the same side of D.

If D is inside C, x and z are outside D, and the connected external endpoint
graph stays outside D. All absorbers of D are strictly inside D. Reverse nesting
is symmetric; disjoint interiors follow from containment alone. Consequently

    Vext(C) intersect Vext(D) is empty for C!=D.        (2)

This argument uses EXTERNALITY in place of the pure proof's exclusion of all
selected phase01 owners. It remains valid when chosen phase10 owners exist,
since those owners are handled separately by J. Each external U has at most
four forced-point roles, by the accepted arbitrary-dimension catalogue, so

    Iext(C)<=4|Vext(C)|,
    sum_C |Vext(C)|=Kext.                              (3)

## A single internal capacity across all chosen cycles

A chosen phase01 source cannot own a forced point anywhere: its terminals
are actual neutral ports; its upper bar corners lie in original full tiles;
all other endpoint roles fail the perpendicular-foreign-neighbor test.

A chosen phase10 source has both upper bar corners used as actual neutral
ports. Neither can be a forced point, whose tile already has at least three
endpoints. The only remaining allowed roles are its two original terminals.
Global forced-point distinctness therefore gives

    J=sum_C J(C)<=2M10.                                (4)

This includes absorption for a DIFFERENT generating curve or multiple contacts
of the same actual owner. Equation(4) is one common occurrence capacity, not
two terminal claims for every cycle. From (3)--(4), with I=sum I(C),

    I<=J+4Kext<=2M10+4Kext.                            (5)

## Exact turning with passive10 corrections

Orient each curve with measured total quarter-turn+4. Use chi_i in{+1,-1}
at its i-th source exit, alternating around the curve. Put beta_i=+1 for an
active exit, -1 for a passive exit. The source arc contributes -2chi_i for
phase01 and zero for phase10; the contact contributes -chi_i beta_i.

Let B01pos and B01neg count passive10 ports adjacent to01 ports, distinguished
by the SIGN OF THEIR SOURCE'S EXIT chi in this orientation. Let B10neg count
passive10 ports adjacent to10 ports with source exit chi=-1. Put
B01=B01pos+B01neg. These signs are source-exit signs, not the chi evaluated at
the passive entry port. Confusing those signs would lose a correction.

The exact summed identity is

    2I+M10+2B01pos = M01+4ell+2B01neg+2B10neg.         (6)

Here is a derivation accounting for10-to-10 contacts. On one curve let E11
and E00 be the numbers of01-to-01 and10-to-10 edges. Degree counting gives
E11=m01-m10+E00. There is exactly one passive10 port on each10-to-10 edge.
If t_e is a contact turn, then summing t_e over incidences at10 sources gives

    sum_(10-incidences) t_e
      = -2 sum_(10 owners) chi_i
        +2 sum_(passive10 ports) chi_owner.

Each10-to-10 edge is counted twice in this incidence sum. Its actual turn
is chi of its passive10 OWNER'S EXIT. Also sum_all chi_i=0, so the total
source-arc turn is +2 sum_(10 owners) chi_i. Cancellation yields

    I(C)-O(C)
      =4-2 sum_(passive10 adjacent01) chi_owner
         -sum_(passive10 adjacent10) chi_owner.

Add I(C)+O(C)=E11 and substitute E00=B10pos+B10neg, where B10pos is the
corresponding positive-sign count. This gives the local form
of (6), which sums over the chosen disjoint components. In particular

    M01+4ell<=M10+2B01+2I.                            (7)

The passive-free case recovers2I+M10=M01+4ell. The unrestricted formula
does not assume there are no10-to-10 edges or no passive10 ports.
