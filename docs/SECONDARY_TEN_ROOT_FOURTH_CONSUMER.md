# Fourth actual root owner: classification and a single four-owner ledger

21 October 2026. Owner: Wenlin Zhang and Haobo Ma. This extends the
[root triple](SECONDARY_TEN_ROOT_DEEP_TRIPLE.md) and the
[reflected cavity exclusions](SECONDARY_TEN_ROOT_CAVITY_EXCLUSION.md).
Every actual original is explicitly assumed to be a full even U or a
standard rotor. This model hypothesis is not an arbitrary-grid catalogue
theorem. The submitted manuscript is unchanged.

## Statement

Use the full H3 chain, n>=2, odd a, even s, even widths u,v>=12,
depth z>=24, and the remaining chain parameters of the root triple.
Write C=s+u+13 and s1=s-v-12. Retain the specified originals

    B=U(u+v+26,z+4,4), chart(a-14+r,s1-1+c);
    R=U(K,8,g), chart(a-9+r,C-1+c), K>=h+2, g>=4;
    A=U(L,4,f), chart(a-8+r,s+1-c),
    6<=L<=v+10, 4<=f<=z-8.

All dimensions are even. In any complete extension of these retained
originals, R's original support p=(a-9,C-1) must have a fresh actual
owner J. Every such owner necessarily has exactly the chart

    J=U(H,D,G), chart(a-10+c,s+u+12-D+r),
    u+L+14<=D<=u+v+22, 6<=H<=z-6, G>=4,           (1)

with even H,D,G and the further bound

    G<=D-u-L-12                         if H<=f+2;
    G<=D-u-18                          if H>=f+4 and H<=12;
    G<=D-u-12                          if H>=f+4 and H>=14.    (2)

Conversely these conditions give endpoint-disjointness from ALL retained
H3,B,R,A, uniformly in K,g and the later blocks. This is geometric
sufficiency; existence of a complete extension is not asserted.

Put N=u+L+12. Relative to H3 alone the single selected-union ledger is

    DeltaS=u+v+L+25, DeltaN=1,
    DeltaF=8+N/2, DeltaE=17+3N/2,
    DeltaPhi=u/2+v+L/2+19>=40.                    (3)

J has immutable charge -1 and marginal 1-N/2. Thus even a remaining
actual-owner branch cannot be handled by assuming nonnegative insertion
marginals. Neither (3) nor the triple's bound54 is monotone under arbitrary
further additions. Both are exact statements about specified unions.

## Exhaustion of J's possible roles

The point p is absent from H3,B,R,A. Its east neighbor lies in R and its
south neighbor lies in P0. It is R's original bar-exterior support, and
two retained occupied neighbors force it to be consumed in a complete
family. Its consuming original must avoid all retained endpoints, so
its own neighbors at p are a nonempty subset of north and west.

The canonical full U endpoint degrees leave only a north terminal, a west
terminal, or the degree-two outer corner with neighbors north and west.
Every north terminal and either corner orientation contains
p+(-4,0)=(a-13,C-1), a B lower-bar endpoint. Those roles are excluded.

There are four fixed guard-compatible rotor tips with own neighbors
north or west. Their complete shapes are the negatives of the four
east/south tip shapes in the cavity table. Two hit B at p+(-2,1)
or p+(-1,1); the other two hit P0 at p+(1,-1) or p+(1,-2).
Non-tip roles already meet an immediate retained neighbor. No rotor
can consume p. Only a west-terminal U remains.

Let b=C-1-D. Its incident row is a-9, containing every column b..C-1.
If D>=u+v+24, that row hits B's near stem at s1. Otherwise b>=s1+2.
A west-terminal U whose bar points upward then reaches B's lower bar
at (a-13,b), because H>=6 and b is within that bar interval. It too is
excluded. Thus the bar points downward, giving exactly J's stated chart,
and parity yields D<=u+v+22.

## Exact necessary and sufficient parameter bounds

J's two-column bar has columns b,b+1 and rows a-9..a+H-10. At row a-8,
A and P0 together occupy the continuous interval [F,C-1], where
F=s+1-L. Since b<C-1, avoidance requires b+1<F, equivalently
D>=u+L+14 by evenness. Hence s1+2<=b<=F-3. Both bar columns lie
strictly between B's near column and A's far column, with separated
physical tile columns.

P1's row-zero bar is at a+z-14, with columns s1+1..s. The two J bar
columns lie in this interval. If H>=z-4 its bar hits P1; evenness gives
H<=z-6. Its last physical tile row then ends at a+z-16, before the
first later-block tile at a+z-15. The near stem lies on row a-9, above
every H3 endpoint, and its column interval misses B's stems and R.

The far row is rF=a+H-10, which is odd, and its rightmost column is
E=b+G, which is even. The following are the first retained endpoints
to the right of b on that row:

| Far-row regime | First retained endpoint column | Required bound on E |
|---|---|---|
| H<=f+2 | A far stem F, an odd column | E<=F-1=s-L |
| H>=f+4, H<=12 | X0 far stem or bar at s-4, even | E<=s-6 |
| H>=f+4, H>=14 | V0 long stem at s+2, even | E<=s |

In the first row rF is within A's far stem; F is the first obstacle
because the interval between B and A is otherwise empty. In the second
row even H is 8,10 or12: X0's first endpoint is s-4 on each of these
three rows, while A's far terminal lies above the physical tile row.
In the third row X0 and the west rotor are above the far tile row; V0's
stem at s+2 continues through it because H<=z-6. Every other first-block
endpoint is still farther right. These bounds are exactly (2).

This proves necessity. It also proves sufficiency: J's bar lies in the
empty, tile-separated two-column corridor, its incident row misses all
old endpoints, its far row stops before the first endpoint in the table,
and its last tile row lies above every later block. B's lower bar lies
above J, its stems outside J's column interval; all R endpoints have
columns at least C. These observations cover every full original,
independently of its unused leg length. The original support p makes
the actual J fresh and distinct from all retained owners.

## A stronger necessary frontier for A

At least one geometrically admissible J exists if and only if

    L<=v+6 OR (L=v+8 AND f<=z-10).                (4)

Indeed (1) first gives L<=v+8, ruling out L=v+10. If H<=f+2, G>=4
and the first cap imply D>=u+L+16, hence L<=v+6. Otherwise H>=f+4
and H<=z-6 imply f<=z-10. If L<=v+6, explicit dimensions
H=6,D=u+v+22,G=4 satisfy all bounds. If L=v+8 and f<=z-10, take
H=f+4,D=u+v+22,G=4. For H<=12 the second cap is at least v+4>=16;
for H>=14 the third cap is v+10>=22. These witnesses prove geometric
existence only. Since completeness requires an actual J, (4) is a
further necessary condition for any complete extension of the prefix.

## Immutable charge and exact physical receipts

For t=(D+G)/2, J has H+t-1 matching edges. Its horizontal two-column
bar is aligned to a physical tile column and meets H/2 tiles; its two
odd-ended horizontal stems add t tiles. Thus T=H/2+t. Its two axial
supports lie in endpoint tiles, its two bar-exterior supports do not,
and the odd-indexed lateral supports contribute t-2. Hence the count
of original supports inside endpoint tiles is t, and

    charge(J)=(H+t-1)+t-2*(H/2+t)=-1.

Charges of older originals remain immutable after supports are occupied.
Only the incident row gives new full mixed tiles or directed foreign
intratile events, with an event excluded whenever its target belongs to
the source's ORIGINAL support set. New full tile minima are exactly

    (a-9,c), c=s+2-L,s+4-L,...,s+u+12,            (5)

giving N/2 tiles. New owner-labelled events are exactly

    J:(a-9,c) -> old:(a-8,c),
      c=s+2-L,s+4-L,...,s+u+12;                 N/2 events;
    A:(a-8,c) -> J:(a-9,c), c=s+1-L,...,s;      L events;
    P0:(a-8,c) -> J:(a-9,c), c=s+1,...,s+u+12; u+12 events.

The opposite parity J directions target original lateral supports.
At the right end both J/R horizontal directions target original axial
or bar-exterior supports. At the left end the tile containing F is
partial. There are TWO essential three-owner tiles in (5):

- At minimum (a-9,s), the upper pair belongs to J; the lower-left
  endpoint belongs to A and the lower-right one to P0.
- At minimum (a-9,C-1), the left pair belongs to J/P0 and the right
  pair to R.

Each of these tiles is absent from either corresponding pairwise union;
count each physical tile once. Pair-only tile inventories miss four
units of the 2F contribution.

There are no additional receipts from the bar: its tile column is
separated from B and A. At the far row the only possibly shared old
tile is the first regime's boundary E=F-1. It remains partial: the cell
(rF-1,E) is empty. Its horizontal directions are excluded by J's axial
support and A's even-indexed lateral support. The X0/V0 obstacles in
the other regimes lie across physical tile-column boundaries. When
H>=f+4, A is above that entire far tile row. All later blocks are
below J's last tile row. This exhausts new mixed full tiles, including
cooperative completions involving several older owners, and events.

Every new tile in (5) contains an endpoint of J absent from the previous
union; each new event has J as an endpoint owner. The new objects are
disjoint from prior receipt sets, and old events persist because supports
are immutable. Adding the new mass1, full tiles N/2 and events3N/2 gives
marginal 1-N/2. Adding this once to the exact triple gain u+v+L+24
gives (3). There is no reuse of any old mass or physical receipt.

## Reproducible witness and verification scope

Take a=31,s=0,n=2,u=v=12,z0=z1=24,h=6,
B=U(50,28,4), R=U(8,8,4), A=U(6,4,4), and
J=U(8,34,4) in the stated charts. All full endpoint sets are disjoint.
The triple gain is54, J's marginal is-14, and the four-owner gain is40.
This is a partial-inventory obstruction to nonnegative marginals;
complete extendibility is not asserted. It is not a counterexample to
a global target inequality.
