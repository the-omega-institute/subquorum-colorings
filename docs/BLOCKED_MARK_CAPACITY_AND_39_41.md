## 1. An improved marked-side capacity lemma

Let B01,B10,B11 count original negative U sources by effective phase and
B=B01+B10+B11. Let K_A count ORIGINAL phase11 upper-bar-corner endpoints
whose own irregular event fires. The established mark assignment uses at
most two original upper corners per phase01 source, two terminal tips per
phase10 source, and the four original corners/tips per phase11 source.
Every assigned endpoint is distinct and is disjoint from every fired
original irregular-event tail. The K_A points are in the phase11 eligible
four-slot set. Therefore the sharper global capacity is

    G+K_A<=2B01+2B10+4B11,
    G<=4B-2B01-2B10-K_A.                           (2)

This is a restriction on mark assignments, not new geometric H reserve.
If r distinguished positive sources inject into the disjoint union of
phase01 source labels and fired phase11 corner labels, then

    r<=B01+K_A<=2B01+2B10+K_A,
    G+r<=4B,
    4Mminus>=9G+9r.                               (3)

The last implication uses Mminus>=9B and sums every original matching once.
No irregular event is counted as an additional H unit, and no negative
charge is substituted for the actual marked-side count G.

## 9. Close the universal counting inequality

Let n0,n1 count unmarked and one-mark positives. Then

    N=n0+n1+z+s+t+p14+p15+q,
    G=n1+2(z+s+t+p14+p15+q),
    mu=n0+R-z-s-t-p14-p15-q.

    Mplus>=9N+5p14+6p15+7q.

Combining with (3) and the original matching partition gives

    4|M|>=36N+20p14+24p15+28q+9G+9r.              (6)

    2ell<=s+p14+p15+r+2q.

Use N>=s+p14+p15+q, R>=0 and G=N+R+ell in (6):

    4|M|>=45s+65p14+69p15+73q+9ell+9r.

    2(45s+65p14+69p15+73q+9r)
