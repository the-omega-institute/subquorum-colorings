Research reproducibility release for *Sub-quorum colorings of hypercubes:
boundary matchings and grid strips*.

- Lean proof of Sahbi's hypercube coloring-number conjecture for every n>=2,
  with pinned original sources and an axiom audit.
- Written boundary Hall matching and signed-product results.
- An exact grafting formula, giving unbounded gaps even on subcubic trees.
- Exact grid-strip certificates at widths 8,9,10,11 for every positive length,
  independently recomputed by two C++ implementations.
- Manuscript PDF, editable LaTeX and full research archives with SHA256 manifests.

The structural/grid extensions are not yet Lean formalized. The manuscript
is a working draft; the arbitrary-width grid conjecture remains open.
The attached verification receipts identify the release commit and completed jobs.
