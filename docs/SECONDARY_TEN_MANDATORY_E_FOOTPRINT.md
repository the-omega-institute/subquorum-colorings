## The arbitrary-incident-depth necessary condition

For general D the right t_(j+1) owner has far-column interval

    [a+z-10-D, a+z-10-D+g] at column C.

It cannot contain any site of K_j, owned by the distinct R_j, or the
retained E endpoint(a,C). Thus the interval avoids ALL rows a-9,...,a.
Both interval endpoints have the same odd parity as a. Avoidance is
equivalent to ending at most a-10 or starting at least a+2. These are
precisely D-g>=z or D<=z-12, proving(1).

R_j is distinct because a geometrically admissible B cannot contain y:
if it contained y then the next row a would also lie in its far column
(its terminal parity is odd, whereas y has even row), colliding with E.
This also covers a bar starting below E; no depth cutoff is assumed.
Only necessity is claimed: old endpoint collisions and contacts can
further restrict either surviving phase.
