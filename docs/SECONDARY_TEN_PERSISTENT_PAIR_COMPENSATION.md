## Exact original-source hypotheses and conclusion

Use the fixed six originals Q,D,L,C,E,W, b even, a=b-9 and even
w>=12, of the [return closure note](SECONDARY_TEN_NORTH_RETURN_CLOSURE.md).
Add T0=U(w+6,6,4) at(a-4+r,w+11-c), and an ACTUAL next left
north-terminal original V=U(k,8,z) at(a-6+r,w+12-c), in either
of the two remaining parameter families:

    k=w+8, 4<=z<=14 even; or k=w+10, z>=16 even.

Let A denote these eight originals. Their endpoint sets are disjoint,
and Phi(A)-Phi(Q/D/W)=-1. Here Phi=N+S+2F-Ev uses the same
ORIGINAL supports and actual directed events throughout; N counts
positive original owners only. No complete realization is assumed.

The persistent ORIGINAL support pair

    e=(a,w+14) in E's original supports,
    t=(a+1,w+14) in C's original supports

is adjacent. Each has its own original endpoint anchor, so both points
are occupied under retention of the original sources, even if later
consumed. Thus both must be consumed in any complete extension.
They are part of the OLD mandatory-support closure, not demands created
by later returns, and they need not have different consumers.

Condition on the actual original consumer at t being either:

1. A right-pointing north-terminal U of arbitrary legal even dimensions
   U(h,ell,f), h>=6, ell,f>=4, in the frame

       J(r,c)=(a+1-ell+r,w+13+c); or

2. The reflected standard rotor Z with map

       Z(r,c)=(a+2-r,w+12+c),

   applied to the standard rotor, also given in the
   [three-site rotor note](SECONDARY_TEN_NORTH_DOUBLE_SITE_ROTOR.md).

In EITHER case the SAME source also consumes e, its endpoints are
disjoint from A, and the exact insertion gives

    Delta N=0, Delta Phi=1,
    Phi(A plus that source)=Phi(Q/D/W).       (1)

This covers BOTH remaining families for EVERY legal dimension stated
above. It is a conditional compensation theorem for these prescribed
original sources. It does not assert that every actual consumer at t
has one of the two displayed forms. Additional exterior originals may
change signed contacts; no global grid bound or unused spare credit is
claimed. The entire one-unit receipt is used once to pay the deficit.

## Proof for every right-pointing U

J's near column is w+14. Its terminal is t and its preceding leg
endpoint is e, since ell>=4. Its far column is w+13+h>=w+19,
beyond every C/E endpoint tile column. Its bar begins at even row
a+1-ell and has leftmost column w+14 even. All other old sources
have endpoint columns at most w+11, apart from E's far column w+13
and C's far column w+14. E's column belongs to the preceding tile
column pair. C's far stem starts at row a+2, strictly below J's
terminal. Its bar begins still further below. These observations also
prove endpoint disjointness for arbitrary ell and arbitrarily long f.

The ONLY shared endpoint tile is(a+1,w+14), containing J's terminal
t and C's far terminal(a+2,w+14). There is no mixed full tile.
Both potential directions are excluded: t is C's original axial support,
and(a+2,w+14) is J's original axial support. Thus Delta F=Delta Ev=0.
This remains true for both residual V families, including arbitrarily
long far legs: their endpoint columns are all at most w+11.

For the original charge put H=(ell+f)/2. The original matching has
h+H-1 edges. Because the two bar rows and its h columns are aligned
in complete tiles, J occupies h/2+H endpoint tiles. Exactly H of its
original supports lie in those tiles: the two axial terminal supports
and the H-2 odd-indexed inward lateral supports. The exterior bar-corner
and even-indexed outward lateral supports occupy other tile columns.
Hence charge(J)=(h+H-1)+H-2(h/2+H)=-1. Its own full bar tiles
are excluded from F by definition, so Delta S=1 and Delta N=0.
Together these identities prove DeltaPhi=1 and (1).

## Proof for the same-owner rotor

The allowed Z has original charge-2 and contains e,t and also E's
original axial support y=(a-1,w+13). All its columns are at least
w+13, whereas T0 and every such V end at or before w+11. Thus it
is endpoint-disjoint from A and has no endpoint tile contact with
either returning source, regardless of V's far length.

It has exactly two shared endpoint tiles with the fixed six sources.
Tile(a+1,w+14) contains t and C's far terminal, with both directions
excluded by original axial supports as above. Tile(a-1,w+12)
contains Z's y and E's far terminal(a,w+13), with no full tile and
exactly one event Z to E. The reverse event targets E's ORIGINAL
axial support y and is excluded. No other old owner meets a Z tile.
Therefore Delta S=2, Delta F=0, Delta Ev=1, Delta N=0, again
giving DeltaPhi=1. E's y need not have been forced at this eight-owner
stage; consuming it is allowed and is not counted as another credit.

## A useful insertion-order identity

In the smallest first residual branch V=T1=U(w+8,8,4), also retain
T2=U(w+10,10,4), placed explicitly by

    T2(r,c)=(a-8+r,w+13-c).

Its original charge is -(w+9), and its insertion into A adds
w/2+5 mixed full tiles and 2w+19 directed events, hence gain0.
The two originals
T2 and Z are endpoint-disjoint. The exact conditional increments are:

| Insertion | Exact DeltaPhi |
| --- | ---: |
| T2 into A | 0 |
| Z into A | 1 |
| Z into A plus T2 | 2 |
| T2 into A plus Z | 1 |

The joint insertion of T2 and Z is2 in either order. The additional
one-unit interaction is from tile(a-1,w+12): only their joint presence
fills it, and they create one additional directed event
T2:(a-1,w+12)->Z:(a-1,w+13). Its reverse targets Z's original support
and is excluded. Thus the mixed second difference is2*1-1=1.
This is an exact finite-set identity, not an additive per-source budget.
Do not claim both Z's later two-unit receipt and a separate full-tile
receipt, or presume T2's marginal stays zero after Z has been inserted.

## The joint-consumer hypothesis cannot simply be removed

For EVERY residual A above, and every even ell>=12, another allowed
original jointly consumes e,t:

    K=U(6,ell,ell-8), K(r,c)=(a+1-ell+r,w+15-c).

Its endpoints are disjoint from A. It is a POSITIVE original owner,
of charge+1, but its exact insertion is

    Delta S=0, Delta N=1, Delta F=0, Delta Ev=1,
    Delta Phi=0.                              (2)

Thus "any one original consumer of both persistent supports repairs
the missing unit" is FALSE in this partial candidate class. This is
an obstacle to that LOCAL rule, not a target counterexample. K's new
positive owner cannot also supply a fresh compensation receipt after
its one unit has paid the original directed contact below.

Proof. Its near column w+14 and terminal t give the same harmless
C terminal tile(a+1,w+14), with both directions excluded. Its far
column is w+9 and its far terminal is at row a-7. The bar lies at
or above a-11 and its far stem ends strictly above V's upper bar
row a-6. No endpoint collision occurs. All old fixed endpoints other
than V and C lie below these possible bar/far contacts or in other
tile columns. The ONLY other shared tile is(a-7,w+8), with K's
far terminal(a-7,w+9) above V's two upper-bar endpoints at row a-6.
It adds no full tile and one event V to K. K's reverse event meets
its original axial far-terminal support and is excluded. This holds
for both residual V widths and all their far lengths, since columns
w+8,w+9 are in V's upper bar for every w>=12.

The bar starts at even row and odd column w+9, ending at even column
w+14. Put H=ell-4=(ell+(ell-8))/2. Its original matching has H+5
edges, it touches H+4 tiles and has H+4 original supports in those
tiles (two bar-corner, two axial terminal and H outward even-indexed
lateral supports). Thus charge(K)=1. The two shared-tile descriptions
give (2) and leave Phi(A plus K)-Phi(Q/D/W)=-1.

K is still incomplete: its far lateral original support
r=(a-7,w+8) is absent, with its own K endpoint east and V endpoint
south. It must be consumed in any complete extension. The failed
joint-pair rule therefore transfers a precise remaining obligation
to the already occupied band; it does not defeat a complete-family
compensation theorem. The subsequent
[narrow-cap exclusion](SECONDARY_TEN_NARROW_CAP_NONCOMPLETION.md) now proves
that this entire K family has NO complete feasible U/rotor extension:
a different forced support p=(a+2,w+12) has no allowed original consumer.
The exact zero increment remains a partial-inventory rule obstruction,
but this particular family cannot occur in the complete model.
