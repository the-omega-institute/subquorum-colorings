## 2. Force E at q

The point q=(b-5,7) is Q's original support (4,7), anchored at its south
neighbor Q(4,6)=(b-4,7). Its west neighbor D(4,1)=(b-5,6) is also occupied.
Thus q must be an endpoint. Neither Q nor D owns it. Its own endpoint
neighbors can only be north/east.

The exact U catalogue leaves a terminal or an outer upper-bar corner;
straight interiors have opposite neighbors and degree-three points are
excluded. Every remaining U role with a north own neighbor continues at
least four steps north (a northward bar continues at least five). It hits
D's lower bar at q+(-3,0)=(b-8,7)=D(1,2). Hence any U consumer is an east
terminal.

There are four possible rotor tips. Both north forms contain
q+(-2,-1)=(b-7,6)=D(2,1). Write the two east footprints, relative to their
tips, as

    ReA={(-2,1),(-1,1),(-1,2),(-1,3),(0,0),(0,1),(0,2),(1,2)},
    ReB={(-1,2),(0,0),(0,1),(0,2),(1,1),(1,2),(1,3),(2,1)}.

ReA hits Q at q+(1,2)=(b-4,9)=Q(2,6), and ReB hits Q at
q+(1,3)=(b-4,10)=Q(1,6). All rotor directions and chiralities are excluded.

For the east U terminal let g be its incident length. D's far endpoint
(b-5,m+5)=D(4,m) exists because t>=4, so the stem must stop before it.
Evenness gives g<=m-4. An upward bar would cross D's lower bar at
(b-8,g+7); the crossing is at the fourth bar position and exists for every
width>=6. Thus the bar points down. If g=4 its lower bar hits Q at
(b-4,10). Therefore g>=6 and its chart is precisely E above.

## 3. Force the negative F and positive T

Let n=(b-6,7). It is simultaneously D's original inner support (3,2),
anchored west at D(3,1), and E's original lateral terminal support,
anchored south at q. It is absent from both endpoint sets. Retention with
two distinct anchors is impossible, so n must be consumed by a third owner.
This owner can have own endpoint neighbors only north/east.

Every north-bearing U role hits D's lower bar two steps north, at
(b-8,7). Both north rotor tips hit D at n+(-2,-1)=(b-8,6). The east ReA
and ReB forms hit E's stem at n+(1,2)=(b-5,9) and n+(1,1)=(b-5,8),
respectively; both points exist since g>=6. Hence the owner is an east
U terminal. Its incident length h obeys h<=m-4 by D(3,m)=(b-6,m+5).
An upward bar crosses D's lower bar at (b-8,h+7), its third bar position.
It must point down and has the stated F chart.

If h<=g, F's bar endpoint (b-5,h+7)=F(0,2) belongs to E's entire
incident row, whose endpoint columns are7,...,g+7. This is a forbidden
different-owner intersection. Therefore h>=g+2. F's width and far leg
do not enter this comparison.

Now p=(b-7,7) is F's original lateral terminal support, anchored south
at n. Its west neighbor D(2,1)=(b-7,6) and north neighbor
D(1,2)=(b-8,7) are endpoints. None of Q,D,E,F owns p. Only an east own
neighbor remains. Thus its consumer is a U east terminal or an east rotor
tip. ReA and ReB hit F's stem at p+(1,2)=(b-6,9) and p+(1,1)=(b-6,8).
Rotors are excluded. D(2,m)=(b-7,m+5) bounds the incident length k by
k<=m-4. An upward bar hits D at (b-8,k+7), its second position; the bar
must point down. This is T. If k<=h, T's bar endpoint
(b-6,k+7)=T(0,2) hits F's incident row. Hence k>=h+2.

The endpoint-owner exclusions precede the collision arguments. E differs
from Q,D because they do not own q. F differs from Q,D,E because they do
not own n. T differs from Q,D,E,F because none owns p. For example E's
minimum endpoint row is b-5, while F's minimum is b-6; p lies above both.
Additional sources and recurring owners elsewhere cannot erase these
original endpoints. No unrelated consumer is assumed fresh.

The E and T charts have even first offsets b-6,b-8 and odd reflection
constants g+7,k+7, giving isolated charge+1. F has first offset b-7 odd
and isolated charge-1, independently of tau,h,xi. Directly, F touches
(tau+h+xi)/2 tiles: tau/2 along its two-row bar and h/2,xi/2 on the
two separate legs. Its original matching has tau-1+(h+xi)/2 edges.
Exactly (h+xi)/2 original supports lie in those touched tiles: the inward
odd-row supports and each beyond-tip support; its outer bar-end supports
and even-row outer supports lie outside. Thus matching+internal-supports
minus twice touched tiles is -1. No resource follows just from introducing F.

E's ordered terminal supports are axial west D(4,1), lateral north F's
terminal n; both are consumed. T's are axial west D(2,1), lateral north
D(1,2), both consumed by D. Each terminal gives ONE activation. The full
E tile contains two D endpoints, F's terminal and E's terminal; the full
T tile contains three D endpoints and T's terminal. No pair/cap/m8 or
second credit for the same terminal is claimed.
