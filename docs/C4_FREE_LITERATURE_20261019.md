# Source-specific context for C4-free representative counting

19 October 2026. This is a limited check, not a completed external
priority search. No claim of novelty for projective-plane dissociation
numbers, the Pappus value or the pair-packing technique is made.

The full accessible HTML of Rafik Sahbi, *Sub-quorum colorings of graphs*,
[arXiv:2609.25128v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.25128v4), was inspected.
Its general degree bound specializes to psi_sq<=rn/(2r-1) for an
r-regular graph, r>=2. Its subcubic discussion gives an exact occupied-set
description, with a matching covering the occupied vertices having
induced degree at least two. That is the same structural criterion
used by our independent finite optimizer. Credit for the representative
reduction and subcubic coloring characterization stays with Sahbi.

The inspected v4 text contains no projective-plane, incidence-graph,
Heawood, Pappus, girth or common-neighbor discussion. This is an observation
about this specific source, not evidence of absence throughout the
literature. The new pair-packing note supplies an additional argument
under C4-free bipartite regularity and an explicit part-size hypothesis.

External discovery attempts were inconclusive: the arXiv search API
returned HTTP429, Google returned a script challenge, and Bing RSS
returned unrelated results for the requested exact phrases. Those
responses provide no usable negative priority evidence. Before a
manuscript novelty statement, inspect relevant work on dissociation,
limited packings and incidence graphs of symmetric designs through
reliable bibliographic records and their primary sources.

The general proof, one-side optimal-support rigidity and explicit
eighteen-vertex sharpness certificate stand independently of that
priority assessment. The graph is the classical Pappus graph; it is
used to establish the sharpness of the current cubic size threshold,
without asserting that its dissociation number was previously unknown.
