## Scoped discovery

Prescribe Q=U(6,8,4), D=U(m,10,t) in the existing charts, condition on
t>=6 and on the ACTUAL first consumer at q=(b-5,7) being the downward
east terminal. The following candidate continuation is worth proving:

    Ei=U(wi,gi,vi), Ei(r,c)=(b-6-i+c,gi+7-r), i=0,...,4,
    6<=g0, gi+2<=g(i+1), g4<=m-4.

The candidate charges alternate +1,-1,+1,-1,+1. In particular m>=18
would be necessary. The likely forcing mechanism is that every lateral
support immediately above Ei has a fixed D endpoint to its west.
At the first further site D's lower bar is four steps north, and at
subsequent sites it is closer. Every north-bearing U role should hit it;
the north rotors should hit D's near stem and east rotors the preceding
incident stem. The far leg t>=6 supplies the width fence at every stage.
This paragraph is a proof plan, not a completed catalogue proof.
