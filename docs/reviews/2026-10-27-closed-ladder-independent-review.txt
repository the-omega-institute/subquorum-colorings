Lemma 5 has the correct factor and disjoint ownershipLet  $\nu$  be the maximum size of a matching between T and H using direct grid edges, and let
$$d=|T|-\nu.$$
For a full  $N$ , let  $a$  count TT components and let  $k$  count TH components containing at least one actual edge. There are
$$|T|-2a$$
TH components altogether. Those without actual edges are necessarily single auxiliary edges, so they form a direct T-to-H matching of size
$$|T|-2a-k.$$
Hence
$$|T|-2a-k\le\nu.$$
Distinct nontrivial TH components contain disjoint, nonempty actual-edge sets. Therefore, if  $p$  counts the distinct actual edges on TH components,
$$p\ge k.$$
Combining the two inequalities gives
$$\boxed{2a+p\ge d}.$$
There is no factor-of-two error and no duplicate charging.
