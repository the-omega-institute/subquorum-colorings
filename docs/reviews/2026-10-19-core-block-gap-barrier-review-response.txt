The weighted calculation is exact:
$$\begin{aligned}

&\ge 17L_1+14L_2\\
&=330r+231b\\
&=\frac{33}{2}(20r+14b)

\end{aligned}$$

$$\boxed{62\beta\ge33n.}$$
There is no double counting. The precise slack identity is
$$31\beta-\frac{33}{2}n

Both terms on the right are nonnegative lower-bound slacks for the same maximum  $\beta$ . No union of the two witnesses is being asserted.With  $g=\mathrm{SF}_2-\beta$ , the accepted compensation inequality gives
