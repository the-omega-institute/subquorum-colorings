Prove or refute this single structural assertion. Let G be a finite simple cubic bipartite graph, S an inclusion-minimal two-star-forming selection, s=|S|, n=|V(G)|, and beta the dissociation number. Suppose 7s=3n+2beta. Must the induced graph on the high selected vertices H={v in S:|N(v) intersect S|>=2} be a disjoint union of cycles, with no path components (isolated high vertices count as paths)? Connected G is the primary case. If false, supply a reproducible graph and selection with exact beta and a complete general/structural proof of its parameter, not just a solver optimum. If true, give a general graph proof, not a sample or unproved enumeration.

Definitions: S forms if each x outside S has either two distinct neighbors in S, or a neighbor u in S that itself has a neighbor in S. Thus non-induced K1,2; deleting v makes v an outside vertex too. Minimal means inclusion-minimal. A dissociation set induces maximum degree at most one. SF maximizes cardinalities of minimal forming sets; here the equality is stated for the actual selection S, not assumed a priori maximum.

Established facts you may use: Every cubic bipartite minimal selection satisfies 7s<=3n+2beta, by the exact two-sided exchange below. Write I for selected isolates (z vertices), P for selected K2 vertices, U for selected leaves with a high support. Choose an injective outside deletion-failure map f:H->T. A nonadjacent high witness has selected degree1 and its sole selected neighbor is a leaf supported only by its high owner (good owner). An adjacent witness has selected degree1 or2; in the latter case its other selected neighbor is in I. Each leaf has a distinct adjacent failure witness of selected degree1or2, with an isolate as the second neighbor when degree2. Overlaps with f(H) can occur only at a degree1 witness owned by that leaf's unique good support.

Put q_v=3-a(f(v))-(a(v)-1)>=0, where a(x)=|N(x) intersect S|. Let r1,r2 count outside vertices not used by f with selected degrees1,2. Let K=|U|-number(good owners). The exact remainder is
W:=3n+2beta-7s=(beta-|D_L|)+(beta-|D_R|)+sum_H q_v+r1+(r1+r2-K).
Here D_L starts with P,U,I_R,H_L and all f-images in R, retaining one leaf at good kept highs and trimming every leaf at bad kept highs; D_R reverses sides. Both are dissociation sets and |D_L|+|D_R|=2s-z-K. All terms in W are nonnegative.

Therefore equality forces q=0,r1=0,K=r2 and both exchanges maximum. High selected-degree2 vertices H2 then have adjacent degree2 witnesses, each with an isolate; selected-degree3 vertices H3 are good with a degree1 witness through a reserved leaf. All other leaves are extra. The high induced graph has degree at most2: at H2 its degree is2 minus its extra leaves, at H3 it is2 minus its extra leaves after reserving one. Hence K=2c, where c is the number of path components of G[H]. Distinct extra leaves use distinct unused witnesses, so equality requires exactly2c unused degree2 witnesses. This does not by itself prove c=0.

The entire zero combinatorial-remainder normal form has S=I,P,H2,H3,U_good,U_extra. A high path endpoint supports one extra leaf (or two for an isolated high). Outside X1 corresponds to H3 through paths H3-U_good-X1. Outside X2_bad corresponds to H2, adjacent to its owner and an isolate. Outside X2_extra corresponds to U_extra, adjacent to its leaf and an isolate. Every leaf has one remaining Y neighbor; P has two Y neighbors; remaining I edges go to Y. Every Y has three S neighbors. The outside graph has degree2 on X1 and degree1 on X2_bad union X2_extra. Thus no capacities are reused. This form is sufficient for minimality; maximum cardinality of the two exchanges is the unresolved part.

Discovery: A connected28-vertex zero-accounting-residual form with one eight-vertex high path, four H2,four H3,two isolates,six leaves,four X1,six X2,two Y has s16,exchange sizes14. In300simple random outside wirings, exact beta was15(64cases)or16(236cases), never14. This is not a general proof. The proposed assertion would explain such strictness. Do not assume from the vanishing arithmetic alone that r2=0: its coefficient is zero in the cubic remainder. Please focus on the graph-level path obstruction, or a genuine equality counterexample. No claim about the full connected asymptotic gap is requested.
