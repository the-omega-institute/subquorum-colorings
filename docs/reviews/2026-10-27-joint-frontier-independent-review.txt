$$\begin{aligned}

   & = O(|V|\,8^s),\\
\text{active table entries}
   & = O(8^s),\\

   & = O(|V|\,8^s).
\end{aligned}$$
There are at most eight possibilities per live row slot and at most four label choices times three actions per state.
