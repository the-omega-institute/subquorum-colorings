The balanced-cell complete-port formula is correctWrite  $Q=V\setminus P=T\mathbin{\dot\cup}H$ , and let  $B_T$  denote the number of black selected terminals. $$x\ge B_T-F_{\max}.$$
Conversely, take a maximum integral flow. At each cell, its marked incoming ports are distinct black vertices: black selected source ports or black endpoints of used actual matching edges.
