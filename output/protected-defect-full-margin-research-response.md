The remaining obstruction to a full proofThe missing step is universal coverage of the demand by these and other nonreused receipts.The local filled-corridor alternative gives
$$v+2\le h\le d-4,$$
but that is not yet a terminating transport theorem. It compares a bar width with one incident leg; the next source's width and other leg remain unrestricted. Iterating it requires a new geometric statement identifying the next configuration and proving that its eventual receipt cannot merge with another demand path.The complete family (19), extended by (27), shows why retaining the old lower pair does not solve that continuation problem: in taller chambers the pair can be filled by a legal complete extension, with an actual reserve loss. The upper-strip theorem survives that operation, but its protected-width hypothesis does not cover every continuation.Thus the advances proved here are:a permanent mixed cap and upper strip at every allowed leg height, with explicit U/rotor exhaustion;nonreuse across certified claims, opposite corners, lower pairs, positive canonical tiles and original terminal receipts;exclusion of the shortest negative-singleton ending, and an activation-or-permanent-pair resolution of the next one;the strengthened aggregate signed-loss bound (1);a complete parametric obstruction to extrapolating the lower-pair rule to taller returns.What is still absent is a proof that the residual negative-U endings always reach enough distinct persistent capacity to satisfy (33), or a complete physical family violating  $\mu\ge0$ . The unrestricted allocation and the broader grid triple equality therefore remain open.
