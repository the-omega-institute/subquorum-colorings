# Exploratory research: long negative corners

The proposals below are research leads. Each requires separate reconstruction and review; this is not an acceptance certificate for the full allocation.

The H argument also produces an all-parameter no-extension stencil.The full allocation inequality remains open. What remains, and how these results change the next stepAfter removing the newly paid tight-Vcorner claims, an unresolved Vcorner must satisfy

The balanced width-six H chamber is paid even when B is wider and the chamber is arbitrarily tall. General long H configurations outside these certificates remain unresolved.The useful change in approach is that four-column chambers should now be tested through their original rotor corner supports, rather than stopped at the endpoint-span test. A rotor that fits geometrically can still force an impossible second consumer. This gives permanent receipts and, in the maximal-far-leg case, a forced equality of leg lengths.For Vcorner, the tight incident endcap reaches C’s intrinsic bar end and produces an activation whose two support owners coincide. That ownership pattern gives a new joint transfer disjoint from the earlier E/S targets, whose two support owners are distinct.I have not established universal coverage of the enlarged inventory, a payment for arbitrary long H or non-tight Vcorner, or a terminating rule for filled corridors and vertical regular exits. No finite-search outcome or execution claim is used in the proofs above.
