Scope of the accepted resultThe proof establishes noncompletion of the prescribed six-source motif by any number of additional original U/rotor sources of arbitrary permitted dimensions. Every reduction uses persistent original endpoints, parity, and the complete-source support rule. Every collision with a named blocker is accompanied by a valid exclusion of that blocker as the consuming owner.The result does not exclude every  $W=10,k=4$  maximal Vcorner configuration, every matched  $f=j+2$  return, or every maximal-width Vcorner configuration. It also asserts no source-count minimality.The negative  $Q$ , the forced partial alternatives, and the eventual contradiction create no positive activation or reserve. The finite searches and reported formal checks are unnecessary to the written proof audited here; this acceptance does not certify their execution.
